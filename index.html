Key features

32.5MP full-frame sensor

About 34% more resolution than the R6 Mark II, which gives more detail and more room to crop.

40 fps with pre-capture

Shoot up to 40 fps with the electronic shutter or 12 fps mechanically. Pre-release capture records frames from just before you press the shutter.

Dual Pixel CMOS AF II

Deep-learning subject detection tracks people, animals and vehicles, and can prioritise people you register in advance.

8.5-stop stabilisation

In-body stabilisation works with lens IS for up to 8.5 stops at the centre of the frame, so handheld shots and video stay steady.

7K open gate video

Record the full sensor area in 7K RAW for reframing to vertical and horizontal formats, or shoot 4K at up to 120p.

Pro-style card slots

One CFexpress Type B and one UHS-II SD slot, plus 10Gbps USB-C, 5GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.1 for fast transfers.

Should I buy the body only or the kit?

If you already own RF lenses, or EF lenses with an adapter, the body only is the better value. The RF 24–105mm f/4L IS USM kit (RRP A$6,149) is a good all-round option if you are starting out with the RF system.

Does it come with a Canon Australia warranty?

Cameras bought from authorised Australian retailers come with Canon Australia warranty support. Grey imports sold by unauthorised sellers may not be covered by Canon Australia.

What’s the difference between the R6 Mark II and R6 Mark III?

The Mark III raises resolution from 24.2MP to 32.5MP, adds a CFexpress Type B slot, 7K open gate video and pre-capture, and improves subject tracking. The body is the same size and only slightly heavier.

What memory cards does the EOS R6 Mark III use?

It has one CFexpress Type B slot and one SD UHS-II slot. CFexpress is recommended for 7K RAW video and long high-speed bursts.

Is the EOS R6 Mark III good for video?

Yes. It records 7K open gate RAW internally, oversampled 4K up to 60p and 4K up to 120p, with 10-bit 4:2:2 and Canon Log 3. It can also output RAW to supported Atomos recorders.

Is the EOS R6 Mark III weather sealed?

Yes. The magnesium alloy body is weather sealed and weighs approximately 699g with battery and card.
